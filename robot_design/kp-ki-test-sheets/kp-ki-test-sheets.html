Kp and Ki Test Sheets

Test it. Measure it. Write it down.
Names:
Date:

Our robot drives straight because the gyro corrects it many times a second. Kp and Ki are the two numbers that decide how hard it corrects. We tuned them on the bare robot. Each attachment changes the weight and balance, so the numbers might need to change. This test finds out. It also gives us proof for the judges: ten runs on paper beat "it seemed fine".

What are Kp and Ki?

Kp reacts to how far off straight the robot is right now. Too low: it drifts. Too high: it weaves left and right.

Ki reacts to how long it has been off. It fixes a steady pull to one side. Too high: it swings past straight and wobbles near the end.

What to do

  1. Set up the course and check the setup list on page 2.
  2. Baseline: run 5 times at the current Kp and Ki. If every run passes, skip to the confirm runs.
  3. Kp sweep: set Ki to 0 and try five Kp values, 3 runs each. Pick the best Kp.
  4. Ki sweep: keep that Kp and try five Ki values, 3 runs each. Pick the smallest Ki that works.
  5. Confirm: 10 runs in a row at the chosen Kp and Ki, then the quick checks and the 30 cm drive.

The one rule

  • Change one number at a time and repeat the exact same test. If two things change, we cannot tell which one made the difference.
  • Write down every run, including the bad ones. A failed run is useful.

Look it's a Giraffe!Kp and Ki Test: Guide

Setup, measuring and what the results mean

Setup list (tick before you start)
Same flat, clean mat or floor every time. Wipe the wheels.
Tape a straight 100 cm line. Mark the end line.
Start jig (two blocks or a wall edge) so every run starts in the same place.
Sticker on the robot's front centre. Always measure to it.
Battery charged. Write the level on the sheet.
Attachment on with quick pins. Tight.
Program is one block: Gyro Move, 100 cm, speed 90.
Hold the robot still for 3 seconds after starting. Do not lift or bump it until it stops.
Roles (swap for each attachment)
RunnerPuts the robot on the jig and starts the program
MeasurerReads the offset and distance error off the tape
RecorderWrites every run on the sheet
CoderChanges Kp or Ki in Gyro Move and downloads to the hub
Wobble score (watch the robot, do not guess later)
0Dead straight
1Tiny twitch
2Visible weave
3Big swings, or the attachment shakes
Where to measure
What does it mean? (Use this when a baseline run misses)
What you seeLikely causeChange first
Ends on the same side every run, no weaveSteady pull: lopsided weight or frictionKi up. Check the attachment is centred.
Weaves left and right the whole wayKp too highKp down
Slowly curves away and Ki does not helpKp too lowKp up
Straight, then swings or wiggles at the endKi too high (it builds up)Ki down

A pass means (suggested target, the team can change it)

  • For 10 runs in a row: every run ends within 2 cm of the line, and the average miss (ignore + and −) is 1 cm or less.
  • Distance error within 1 cm, wobble 1 or lower, and no problem runs (attachment shifts, robot tips, seeds fall out).
  • The misses are not all on the same side. All left means drift, not luck.
