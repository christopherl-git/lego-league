# Innovation Project

Materials for the FLL Innovation Project — the judged category where the team identifies a real-world problem related to the season theme and designs a solution.

## Contents

- [topic-vote-2026-10-06.md](topic-vote-2026-10-06.md) — topic presentations, observations, and voting method
- [topic-vote-tracker.xlsx](topic-vote-tracker.xlsx) — top-3 vote entry for 9 students with automatic tally and winner
- [invasive-species-research.md](invasive-species-research.md) — running list of invasive species researched, what's been tried, sources and our solution ideas
- [project-plan.md](project-plan.md) — week-by-week plan to the 2026-11-28 tournament, solution options and roles
- [solution-scoring-worksheet/solution-scoring-worksheet.pdf](solution-scoring-worksheet/solution-scoring-worksheet.pdf) — 6-page printable in the team worksheet style for choosing the solution (2026-11-10): scoring guide (New, Doable, Biodiversity, 1 to 5), personal score sheet for options A to F plus two new ideas, a new-idea page with sketch box, team tally and "Our choice", and four expert feedback boxes. Print page 2 for each member. The `.html` is its source.
- [expert-email-worksheet/expert-email-worksheet.pdf](expert-email-worksheet/expert-email-worksheet.pdf) — 4-page printable of Ella's expert email template: one page each for Bob Leigh, Dan Panko and Matthew Lusty, plus a blank page for a new expert. Each page has space to write three questions, a sent/reply tracker, and an "About" box with what we know about the expert and ideas to ask. The `.html` is its source. Filled-in scan: [expert-questions-2026-11-10.pdf](expert-email-worksheet/expert-questions-2026-11-10.pdf).
- [expert-emails-2026-11-10.md](expert-emails-2026-11-10.md) — ready-to-send email drafts for Bob Leigh, Dan Panko, Matthew Lusty and Pete (firefighter): Ella's template, the chosen solution, and the team's questions. Replies so far: Bob (2026-11-10) and Pete (2026-11-12)
- [drone-comparison-worksheet/drone-comparison-worksheet.pdf](drone-comparison-worksheet/drone-comparison-worksheet.pdf) — 8-page printable comparing 10 3D-printable drones from MakerWorld (picture, facts, description). Each member scores carrying capacity, speed, distance and "can we build it?" (1 to 5), ticks the best job (scout or water drone) and writes what we would modify; then picks a best scout and best water drone, with a team tally. The `.html` is its source; pictures are in `images/`.
- **Expert feedback 2026-11-10:** Bob Leigh replied; we now plan **two drones**, a light, fast scout with a heat camera and LIDAR, and a bigger water drone, with a person checking before water is dropped. See [project-plan.md](project-plan.md#expert-feedback-bob-leigh-replied-2026-11-10).
- **Solution chosen 2026-11-10:** map the grass, then send a winged drone (99 of 135). Team scores scan: [team-scores-2026-11-10.pdf](solution-scoring-worksheet/team-scores-2026-11-10.pdf); results are in [project-plan.md](project-plan.md#solution-chosen-2026-11-10).
