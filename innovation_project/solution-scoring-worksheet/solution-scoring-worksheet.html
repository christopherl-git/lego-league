Our problem

Invasive grasses (Guinea, Gamba, Molasses and Cogon) dry out and burn. The fires kill rainforest plants, and the grass grows back first, so every fire lets the grass spread further into the rainforest. Our solution has to help break this grass–fire cycle.

What to do

  1. Read the scoring guide below so we all score the same way.
  2. Score each idea on your own on page 2. Give each idea 1 to 5 points in each column. Use the research, not just what sounds cool.
  3. Got a new idea? Write it on page 3 and score it the same way.
  4. Add up as a team on page 4. Then talk about the top two before we vote.
  5. Ask an expert. Write what they tell us on pages 5 and 6, and what we changed because of it.

Scoring guide

Score it on ...1 No3 Some5 Yes!
NewIs it innovative?Someone already does this, the same way.Uses something that exists, but in a new way or a new place.Nobody does this yet. We can say what is new.
DoableCould it really work?Too big, too costly or too risky. We can't explain how it works.Could work with more research. We can draw it.Could really work in a rainforest, and we can show it with a model by 11-17.
BiodiversityDoes it help plants and animals?Doesn't help, or harms native plants, animals or soil.Helps one part: stops the grass or stops the fires.Breaks the whole cycle and protects lots of rainforest life.

Use 2 and 4 for in between. The highest total is 15.

Judges will ask

  • What solutions already exist, and how is ours different?
  • How does your solution help biodiversity?
  • Who gave you feedback, and what did you change because of it?
