## Brown tree snake (Guam)

- **Problem:** kills native animals in Guam
- **Already tried:** bait mice with poison in them, snake-sniffing dogs, water testing. The snakes are still there.
- **Our ideas:** a better trap; keep other animals out of it; a camera that recognizes the snake and opens a door
- **What we found:** snake traps already exist and use a one-way door. A camera would cost more and work less well.
- **Sources:** USGS fact sheet; USDA National Wildlife Research Center; University of Washington (traps)

## Dog-strangling vine (Ontario)

- **Problem:** grows in thick mats that choke out native plants, and its roots release a chemical that kills other plants. Native to Europe. Toxic to humans.
- **Already tried:** chemicals (also kill other plants); digging (works but hard); goats grazing in a fenced-off area
- **How to remove it:** dig out the whole deep root without snapping the stem. Don't put it in the garbage or compost, where it regrows. Bag it in a thick garbage bag. It has to be dried out to die.
- **Our ideas:** a robot digger; drones that place boxes over the plants to block their sunlight
- **Sources:** Invasive Species Centre (.ca); Ontario Invasive Plant Council (.ca); Ottawa Field-Naturalists' Club (.ca); watersheds.ca; PEI Invasive Species Council (peiinvasives.com)

## Chosen problem: invasive grass and rainforest fires (2026-10-27)

- **Problem:** grass dries up and causes fires, and the fires let more grass take over, so it turns into a cycle (Jordan). Roads make room for invasive grass to spread into rainforests.
- **Our ideas:** drones that put out fires and also detect them; drones that drop sand; map and monitor the invasive grass; herbicide on the grass beside roads
- **Sources:** Sylvie's two YouTube videos on drones carrying liquid-filled balls to put out fires (see [2026-10-27 notes](../team/resources/2026-10-27/meeting-notes.md)). Still needed: credible written sources for the grass–fire cycle.

## Miconia: more from 2026-10-27

- Blocks sunlight and spreads quickly, so other plants can't get light; the roots cause erosion (Alexis, with lots of sources still to add)
- **Our ideas (not chosen):** a robot that eats the tree; a robot that spreads caterpillars; modified worms that eat the roots. No good idea for the erosion problem.
