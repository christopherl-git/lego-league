# Team Meeting Notes — 2026-09-22

## Robot design review and scoring

The team reviewed the four candidate base robot designs together and scored each one in the scoring workbook.

Materials used, both saved in this folder:

- [robot-design-scoring-deck.pptx](robot-design-scoring-deck.pptx) — images of each robot with prompts for what to look at
- [robot-design-scoring.xlsx](robot-design-scoring.xlsx) — our scores are on the Scoring tab

The four designs:

| Design | Source | Instructions |
|---|---|---|
| Comp Bot | Zain Khan ("Coach Zain") | free — `build instructions/comp bot.pdf` |
| HummerOne | Next Level Teacher | free with course signup — `build instructions/HummerOne.pdf` |
| StarterBot | Robotics Rules | $30, not purchased |
| GummyBears / Droid Bot M | GummyBears Robotics + the Seshan Brothers | free — `fllrobotjan2025.pdf`, `DroidBotMSpikePrime.pdf` |

Scored on six things, all about how a robot performs in a match: archetype match, attachment swap speed, squaring against the wall, stability under load, maneuverability, and drive repeatability. Cost, build time and parts count were deliberately kept out of the scoring and treated as a separate decision afterwards.

GummyBears and Droid Bot M were scored as one design — the two published builds produce near-identical robots.
