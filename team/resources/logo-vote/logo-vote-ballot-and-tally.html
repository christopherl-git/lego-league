Team Logo Ballot

Rank the logos. Your 1 is your favourite.
  1. Write 1 in the box next to your favourite logo, 2 next to your second favourite, then 3, and so on.
  2. Rank as many logos as you like. Leave a box empty if you really don't want that logo.
  3. Use each number only once. You may vote for your own logo.
  4. Don't write your name. Fold your ballot in half and hand it in.

Why rank instead of picking one?

  • If your favourite gets the fewest votes, your vote isn't wasted. It moves to your next choice.
  • The winner is the logo that more than half of the team is happy with.

Counting the votes

  1. Round 1: sort the ballots into piles by the logo marked 1. Write each pile's count in the Round 1 column.
  2. If a logo has more than half of the ballots still counting, it wins. Stop and fill in the winner box.
  3. If not, the logo with the fewest votes is out. Move each of its ballots to that ballot’s next choice still in the race. If a ballot has no choices left, put it in the “No choices left” row.
  4. Count again in the next round. Repeat until one logo has more than half.

Tie for fewest votes? The one with fewer 1st-choice votes in Round 1 is out. Still tied? Flip a coin.  Spoiled ballot (a number used twice)? Count it only up to the mistake.
