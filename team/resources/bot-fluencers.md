## Zain Khan ("Coach Zain")
- YouTube: https://www.youtube.com/@ZainKhan-cg7ln (3K subscribers, 37 videos — FLL, FTC, EV3 and CAD)
- Found: researching starter robot designs (2026-09-22) — not presented by a team member
- Starter bot ("Comp Bot"): "5 Simple Tips to Build the Ultimate FLL Robot" (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4aHr97Xof34) — 91K views, by far the most watched build video we found. Free PDF instructions: https://drive.google.com/file/d/18biuDIcTEnGeycvEj1K0PnMxgbRZ-bfL/view — this is our `build instructions/comp bot.pdf`
- The five tips: (1) choose components deliberately, (2) build outward from the drivetrain, (3) stabilise the design, (4) build a frame around the robot, (5) use dog gears for attachment coupling
- Design: fully enclosed box robot, 225 steps and 158 of them framing. Drive wheels sit entirely inside the frame, hub recessed into the top deck, two attachment motors with dog-gear couplings. Needs 4 motors — probably more than one core set, check inventory first
- Also worth watching — SPIKE Prime + Python, directly useful for our PID code in `robot_design/`:
  - Drive perfectly straight in Python: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=H88PJ9W45Qk
  - Turn perfectly using the gyro in Python: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aiLNSHbeILM
  - Follow a line perfectly in Python: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sMH2cS3sfNA
  - Intro to programming SPIKE Prime in Python: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KdrNlSSvb4k
  - (Word Blocks versions of all four exist too and are his most-watched videos)
- First to implement: TBD — the Python drive-straight video is the obvious first one for Sylvie, Brynn and Jack
- Status: not chosen as our base robot (2026-09-22) — scored 20.5/30 in the team's design scoring, second of four. His SPIKE Prime Python videos stay on the list regardless of which robot we build
